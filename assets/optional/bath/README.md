# Diziet bath patch

Requires MyPreciouses 1.0.0 and
[Diziet's Player Home Bath Undressing](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/30265)
7.1.2.7. Install after both mods and let this patch overwrite
`Scripts/dz_undress_player_effect_script.pex`.

Restores the player's extra rings to their fingers after bathing, preserving
custom enchantments and names. Rings removed from inventory are skipped, and new
selections take priority. Saved equipment survives saving inside the bath. The
bath mod's slow unequip option continues to leave extra rings equipped.

The replacement script is based on Diziet's script, with credits to
psychoslaphead, Ac3s and subtanker. Its reuse permission is published under
Permissions and credits on the bath mod's page.
